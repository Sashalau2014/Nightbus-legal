NightBus（夜航）是一款提醒與家庭陪伴 App。我們只收集讓 App 運作所必需的最少資料，分享多少由你決定。

我們收集什麼

  • 匿名帳號識別碼 — 第一次打開 App 時，由 Firebase 匿名登入產生的隨機 ID。無需註冊，我們不會要求你的真實姓名、電郵或電話。
  • 你自行輸入的內容 — 暱稱、提醒名稱與時間、上車紀錄，以及副駕送出的訊號。選擇「我是駕駛員」之後，這些內容會同步到雲端，用於重裝或換機時找回紀錄。配對之後，副駕能讀到多少，由你選的分享模式決定（見下方「分享由你控制」）。
  • 推播通知 token — 用來送達通知的裝置識別碼。App 啟動時，Firebase Cloud Messaging SDK 可能會向 Google 取得 token；只有在你允許通知之後，我們才會把它存到你的帳號下，你關閉通知後下一次打開 App 時移除。它只用於兩種通知：副駕送給駕駛員的訊號，以及駕駛員連續兩班未上車時給副駕的提醒。
  • 還原狀態 — App 把紀錄還原到裝置時，會記下結果、錯誤原因（如有）與 App 版本，用來排除同步問題。
  • Apple 帳號綁定（選用） — 只用於重裝或換機時找回紀錄。我們透過 Sign in with Apple 取得識別碼與你允許的姓名、電郵（可選「隱藏我的電郵」）。

我們不收集什麼

  • 不收集用藥劑量、診斷或任何醫療資訊——提醒名稱由你自由命名，App 沒有任何醫療欄位。
  • 不收集位置、聯絡人、照片。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤、沒有第三方分析，絕不出售資料。

資料存放

同步的資料儲存於 Google Firebase 位於美國的資料中心，傳輸全程加密；我們的伺服器函數在台灣（asia-east1）執行。

伺服器函數只做三件事：一個排程函數（每 15 分鐘）為寬限已過而手機未打卡的班次補記「未上車」，並在連續兩班未上車時更新總覽的警示旗標；另外兩個函數負責送出上面提到的兩種通知。除此之外不處理你的資料。

分享由你控制

存取規則在伺服器端強制執行，只有你本人和配對的副駕能讀取。駕駛員可隨時切換三種模式：

  • 詳細廣播 — 副駕可以看到每個班次的時間與上車紀錄。
  • 只看總覽 — 副駕只看到今天的總覽與是否塞車，看不到個別班次。
  • 靜音模式 — 副駕讀不到你的班次、紀錄與總覽，也不會收到塞車提醒。

你也可以解除配對——解除後副駕即無法再看到任何資料。

刪除資料

在 App 內「設定 → 清除所有資料」會同時刪除雲端與這台手機上的資料：你的帳號紀錄、你的配對與其下所有班次、上車紀錄、總覽與訊號、推播 token，以及匿名帳號本身。此動作無法復原；沒有網路時不會刪除任何東西。

  • 如果你綁定了 Apple 帳號，App 會先請你向 Apple 確認，並撤銷綁定。
  • 如果你是副駕，清除會刪掉你自己的紀錄並與駕駛員解除連結；駕駛員的資料仍屬於駕駛員。
  • 伺服器的運作紀錄檔可能含有你的隨機 ID，不在上述刪除範圍內，會依 Google Cloud 的保留期限自動過期。

無法使用 App 時，可來信 linkedheart.app@gmail.com，我們會代為刪除。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

NightBus is a reminder and family-companion app. We collect the minimum needed for the app to work, and you decide how much is shared.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier — a random ID created by Firebase anonymous sign-in the first time you open the app. No registration; we never ask for your real name, email or phone number.
  • Content you enter — your nickname, reminder names and times, check-in records, and the signals a co-pilot sends. Once you choose "I'm the pilot", this syncs to the cloud so your records can be recovered after a reinstall or on a new device. After you pair, how much your co-pilot can read depends on the sharing mode you choose (see "Sharing is yours to control").
  • A push notification token — a device identifier used to deliver notifications. The Firebase Cloud Messaging SDK may obtain a token from Google when the app starts; we store it with your account only after you allow notifications, and remove it the next time the app opens after you turn them off. It is used for two notifications only: a co-pilot's signal to the pilot, and a heads-up to the co-pilot when the pilot has gone two runs in a row without checking in.
  • Restore status — when the app restores your records onto a device, it saves the outcome, an error reason if there was one, and the app version, so sync problems can be fixed.
  • Apple ID linking (optional) — used only to recover your records after a reinstall or on a new device. Through Sign in with Apple we receive an identifier and the name and email you allow (you may choose "Hide My Email").

What we do not collect

  • No dosages, diagnoses, or any medical information — reminder names are free text you choose; the app has no medical fields.
  • No location, contacts, or photos.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we never sell data.

Where data lives

Synced data is stored in Google Firebase data centers in the United States and is encrypted in transit; our server functions run in Taiwan (asia-east1).

Our server functions do three things: a scheduled function (every 15 minutes) records when a run's grace period has passed without a check-in from the phone and updates the summary's alert flag after two such runs in a row; and two functions send the notifications described above. They do nothing else with your data.

Sharing is yours to control

Access rules are enforced on the server: only you and your paired co-pilot can read your data. The pilot can switch between three modes at any time:

  • Full broadcast — your co-pilot sees each run and its boarding record.
  • Overview only — your co-pilot sees today's overview and traffic status, not individual runs.
  • Silent mode — your co-pilot cannot read your runs, records or overview, and receives no in-traffic notification.

You can also unpair — after which the co-pilot can no longer see anything.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Erase all data deletes your data both in the cloud and on this phone: your account record, your pair and every run, check-in, summary and signal under it, your push token, and the anonymous account itself. This cannot be undone; without an internet connection nothing is erased.

  • If you linked an Apple ID, the app asks you to confirm with Apple first and revokes the link.
  • If you are a co-pilot, erasing removes your own record and detaches you from the pilot; the pilot's data remains theirs.
  • Server operation logs may contain your random ID. They are not covered by this deletion and expire under Google Cloud's log retention.

If you cannot use the app, email linkedheart.app@gmail.com and we will delete your data for you.
