隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 20 日

Nightbus（夜航）是一款提醒與家庭陪伴 App。我們的原則很簡單：只收集讓 App 運作所必需的最少資料，分享多少永遠由你決定。

我們收集什麼

  • 匿名帳號識別碼 — 由 Firebase 匿名登入產生的隨機 ID。無需註冊，我們不收集你的姓名、電郵或電話。
  • 你自行輸入的內容 — 提醒名稱與時間、完成紀錄、暱稱、自訂獎勵與懸賞文字。這些內容只在你與守護者配對之後，依你選擇的分享模式同步到雲端，供對方查看。未配對時，一切只存在你的裝置上。
  • Apple 帳號綁定（選用） — 只用於重裝或換機時找回紀錄。我們透過 Sign in with Apple 取得一個識別碼；你可以選擇隱藏電郵。

我們不收集什麼

  • 不收集用藥劑量、診斷或任何醫療資訊——提醒名稱由你自由命名，App 沒有任何醫療欄位。
  • 不收集位置、聯絡人、照片。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤、沒有第三方分析，絕不出售資料。

資料存放

配對同步的資料儲存於 Google Firebase（Firestore 與 Authentication，asia-east1 區域），傳輸全程加密。存取規則保證只有配對雙方能讀取自己配對內的資料。

分享由你控制

駕駛員可隨時切換「詳細廣播 / 只報平安 / 靜音」三種分享模式，或解除配對——解除後守護者即無法再看到任何資料。

刪除資料

在 App 內「設定 → 清除所有資料」可移除本機所有紀錄並登出帳號。如需刪除雲端資料，請來信 calabocahas2hearts@gmail.com，我們會刪除你的帳號與相關文件。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Nightbus is a reminder and family-companion app. Our principle is simple: we collect the minimum needed for the app to work, and you always decide how much is shared.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier — a random ID created by Firebase anonymous sign-in. No registration; we do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • Content you enter — reminder names and times, completion records, nicknames, and custom reward/bounty text. This syncs to the cloud only after you pair with a guardian, under the sharing mode you choose. Unpaired, everything stays on your device.
  • Apple ID linking (optional) — used only to recover your records after a reinstall or on a new device, via Sign in with Apple. You may hide your email.

What we do not collect

  • No dosages, diagnoses, or any medical information — reminder names are free text you choose; the app has no medical fields.
  • No location, contacts, or photos.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we never sell data.

Where data lives

Paired data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication, asia-east1 region) and encrypted in transit. Security rules ensure only the two paired accounts can read their own pair's data.

Sharing is yours to control

The pilot can switch between Full broadcast / All-clear only / Silent at any time, or unpair — after which the guardian can no longer see anything.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Erase all data removes everything on the device and signs the account out. To delete cloud data, email calabocahas2hearts@gmail.com and we will delete your account and its documents.
