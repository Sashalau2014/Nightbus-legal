隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日

Nightbus（夜航）是一款提醒與家庭陪伴 App。我們的原則很簡單：只收集讓 App 運作所必需的最少資料，分享多少永遠由你決定。

我們收集什麼

  • 匿名帳號識別碼 — 由 Firebase 匿名登入產生的隨機 ID。無需註冊，我們不收集你的姓名、電郵或電話。
  • 你自行輸入的內容 — 提醒名稱與時間、完成紀錄、暱稱。這些內容在你完成初次設定後，同步到雲端一條只有你本人可讀的路徑，用於重裝或換機時找回紀錄；配對後，副駕依你選擇的分享模式可讀其中一部分。
  • Apple 帳號綁定（選用） — 只用於重裝或換機時找回紀錄。我們透過 Sign in with Apple 取得一個識別碼；你可以選擇隱藏電郵。

匿名使用時不收集姓名、電郵；綁定 Apple 帳號時由 Apple 提供，可選隱藏電郵。

我們不收集什麼

  • 不收集用藥劑量、診斷或任何醫療資訊——提醒名稱由你自由命名，App 沒有任何醫療欄位。
  • 不收集位置、聯絡人、照片。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤、沒有第三方分析，絕不出售資料。

資料存放

同步的資料儲存於 Google Firebase（Firestore 與 Authentication，asia-east1 區域），傳輸全程加密。存取規則保證只有你本人——以及配對後的副駕，依分享模式——能讀取。一個排程伺服器函數（每 15 分鐘）為寬限已過而手機未打卡的班次補記「未上車」，並在連續兩班未上車時更新總覽的警示旗標；除此不寫任何東西。

分享由你控制

駕駛員可隨時切換「詳細廣播 / 只報平安 / 靜音」三種分享模式，或解除配對——解除後副駕即無法再看到任何資料。

刪除資料

在 App 內「設定 → 清除所有資料」可移除本機所有紀錄並登出帳號。如需刪除雲端資料，請來信 linkedheart.app@gmail.com，我們會刪除你的帳號與相關文件。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Nightbus is a reminder and family-companion app. Our principle is simple: we collect the minimum needed for the app to work, and you always decide how much is shared.

What we collect

  • An anonymous account identifier — a random ID created by Firebase anonymous sign-in. No registration; we do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • Content you enter — reminder names and times, completion records, nicknames. After onboarding this syncs to a cloud path readable only by you, so your records can be recovered after a reinstall or on a new device; once you pair, your co-pilot can read part of it under the sharing mode you choose.
  • Apple ID linking (optional) — used only to recover your records after a reinstall or on a new device, via Sign in with Apple. You may hide your email.

Used anonymously, we collect no name or email; if you link your Apple account, Apple provides them and you can hide your email.

What we do not collect

  • No dosages, diagnoses, or any medical information — reminder names are free text you choose; the app has no medical fields.
  • No location, contacts, or photos.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we never sell data.

Where data lives

Synced data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication, asia-east1 region) and encrypted in transit. Security rules ensure only you — and, once paired, your co-pilot under your sharing mode — can read it. A scheduled server function (every 15 minutes) writes a "missed" check-in for any slot whose grace period has passed without a check-in from the phone, and updates the summary's alert flag when two consecutive slots are missed. It writes nothing else.

Sharing is yours to control

The pilot can switch between Full broadcast / All-clear only / Silent at any time, or unpair — after which the co-pilot can no longer see anything.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Erase all data removes everything on the device and signs the account out. To delete cloud data, email linkedheart.app@gmail.com and we will delete your account and its documents.
