1. 快速開始

Nightbus 有兩個角色，通常在兩台手機上使用：

  • 駕駛員——記錄自己日常班次（提醒）的人。點火打卡、賺里程、換獎勵。
  • 塔台——守護駕駛員的家人或夥伴。只看得到駕駛員選擇分享的內容，可以發佈懸賞、批准兌換、送出無聲的鼓勵。
  1. 從 TestFlight 安裝 Nightbus，打開 App——免註冊，直接開始。
  2. 在「你在哪一邊？」選擇你的角色（選一次就好，之後可在設定切換）。
  3. 駕駛員：輸入你的代號（例如 Casper）就出發。塔台：輸入駕駛員給你的 6 位配對碼。
  4. 駕駛員記得在提示出現時開啟通知——班次到站的提醒全靠它。
想先看看塔台長什麼樣？塔台的配對碼頁有「示範模式」，單機就能預覽完整守航儀表板，不需要配對。

2. 駕駛員指南

航線（首頁）——點火

每個班次到站時，回到 App 按下引擎點火。獎勵按時機計算：

時機狀態里程
到站後 15 分鐘內準時點火+50 哩
15 分鐘後、拋錨前遲到點火+20 哩
拋錨後補登補登點火+10 哩

狀態不佳？按「塞車中」可延後 15 分鐘，每班最多兩次，塞車期間塔台不會收到通知。超過到站 1 小時（塞車會順延）仍未點火，班次「拋錨」——如果你開著詳細廣播，塔台會收到一次拋錨警報。

燃料艙（設定）——管理班次

  • 新增班次：取個自己的叫法（例如「專心燃料」）、挑圖示顏色、加一個或多個時間（12 小時制）。
  • 每日循環：每天同一時間出班，直到你刪除。僅今天：只出今天一班，明天自動下架——適合一次性的事。
  • 新帳號自帶兩個示範班次（專心燃料、晚安補給），名稱時間隨你改，不需要也可以刪。

長途客運——專注計時

選 15／25／50 分鐘開出一班「不靠站的高速巴士」，全螢幕巡航動畫＋可開關的雨聲白噪音。撐到終點每分鐘 +4 哩；中途回廠則不計里程。

支線任務——待辦拆站

把日常待辦拆成小站（+10）、中站（+20）、大站（+40），完成即得里程。超過 25 分鐘的事，建議拆站或開一班長途客運。

補給站——花掉你的里程

  • 黑市：上架你想要的現實特權（例如「晚睡一小時」）並自己定價。按「兌換」後扣里程並送塔台批准——批准才生效，婉拒則里程退回。
  • 懸賞榜：塔台發佈的挑戰。達標後「領賞」；覺得獎賞不夠好，可以「反提議」議價——談判是你的權利。
  • 車庫：解鎖巴士塗裝——暗夜紫電（連續 3 天）、賽博粉紅（連續 7 天）、像素螢光（5 班長途）、幽浮銀翼（總里程 5,000 哩）。

訊號廣播——分享多少你說了算

模式塔台看到什麼
詳細廣播每個班次的時間與點火紀錄、拋錨警報
只報平安只有「今日 x/y ✓」，看不到細節
靜音模式什麼都收不到（包括警報）

保留你的紀錄（選用）

設定裡可用 Apple 帳號一鍵綁定。綁定後換手機也能找回配對、航線和里程。不綁定也能正常使用。

3. 塔台指南

  • 守航儀表板：對方的今日完成數、連續天數、總里程；開詳細廣播時還有完整時間軸。班次拋錨會出現紅色警報，按「收到」即可。
  • 無聲的默契：三個極簡訊號——「+1 酷」「RESPECT」「收到 COPY」。一個 +1 就夠了：對方知道你在，但不煩。
  • 懸賞任務：按「發佈」設定獎勵＋目標。目標可選連續航行／長途客運／準時點火（量化自動計進度），或自訂任務（自由填寫內容，例如「星期天洗碗」，由對方完成後自行領賞，你看到「已完成」再兌現）。對方反提議時，你可以接受新獎賞或維持原案。
  • 補給站審批：對方上架的特權會即時顯示（已上架）；對方按兌換後變成待批准——批准即生效，婉拒則退回里程。說到做到。
塔台的精神是「安靜守護」：你能做的刻意有限——不能改對方的班次、不能留言說教。給空間也是一種信任。

4. 配對

  1. 駕駛員：設定 → 塔台連線 → 產生配對碼，把 6 位數字給你的塔台。
  2. 塔台：輸入暱稱（例如「媽媽」）和配對碼，按連接。
  3. 配對碼一次性、24 小時有效；重新產生會使舊碼立即失效。
  4. 任一方可隨時解除配對。塔台解除後，駕駛員可再產生新碼；駕駛員解除則整條配對結束。

5. 身分切換與夥伴模式

塔台不是家長專屬——互相督促的同學、朋友也可以當彼此的守護者。

  • 你可以同時是自己的駕駛員和別人的塔台——兩個視圖隨時切換，互不影響。
  • 駕駛員視圖：設定 → 守航 → 切到守航視圖。塔台視圖：頁面底部「切到我的駕駛艙」。
  • 唯一限制：不能當自己的塔台——互相守護需要各自的裝置、互發配對碼。

6. 常見問題

收不到提醒通知？

檢查 iOS 設定 → 通知 → Nightbus 是否允許。提醒會預先排程約一週；久未開 App 會收到「提醒已休眠」通知，開一下 App 就會續上。

換手機或刪除重裝會怎樣？

同一台裝置重裝通常會自動回到原帳號。想跨裝置保險，請在設定用 Apple 帳號綁定。

想整個重來？

設定 → 清除所有資料：移除本機紀錄並登出，重開後從角色選擇重新開始。

對方的操作多久會出現在我這邊？

配對雙方之間的同步通常 5–20 秒，視網路而定。

我的資料安全嗎？

免註冊、匿名帳號；只在配對後同步你選擇分享的內容；無廣告、無追蹤、不出售資料。詳見隱私政策。

1. Quick start

Nightbus has two roles, normally on two phones:

  • Pilot — the one tracking their own daily runs (reminders). Ignite on time, earn mileage, spend it on rewards.
  • Tower — the family member or buddy watching over the pilot. Sees only what the pilot chooses to share; can post bounties, approve redeems, and send silent encouragement.
  1. Install Nightbus from TestFlight and open it — no sign-up, you're in.
  2. On "Which side are you on?", pick your role (once; you can switch later in Settings).
  3. Pilot: enter a callsign (e.g. Casper) and go. Tower: enter the 6-digit code your pilot gives you.
  4. Pilots: enable notifications when asked — arrival reminders depend on it.
Curious what the Tower side looks like? The tower's code-entry screen has a Demo mode — a full sample dashboard on one device, no pairing needed.

2. Pilot guide

Route (home) — ignition

When a run arrives, open the app and hit IGNITE. Rewards depend on timing:

WhenStatusMileage
Within 15 min of arrivalOn time+50 mi
After 15 min, before breakdownLate ignition+20 mi
After breakdownLogged late+10 mi

Not feeling it? Tap "In traffic" to delay 15 minutes, up to twice per run — the tower is not notified while you're in traffic. If a run goes 1 hour past arrival (delays extend this) without ignition, it breaks down — with Full broadcast on, the tower gets one breakdown alert.

Fuel bay (Settings) — manage runs

  • Add a run: name it whatever you like (e.g. "Focus Fuel"), pick an icon and colour, add one or more times (12-hour).
  • Every day: departs at the same time daily until you delete it. Today only: runs once and retires itself tomorrow — for one-off things.
  • New accounts come with two sample runs (Focus Fuel, Goodnight Supply) — rename, retime, or delete them freely.

Express coach — focus timer

Depart a non-stop express of 15 / 25 / 50 minutes: full-screen cruising animation with optional rain sounds. Reach the terminus for +4 mi per minute; return early and no mileage is counted.

Side quests — split your to-dos

Break tasks into small (+10), mid (+20) and big (+40) stops — every stop earns mileage. Anything over 25 minutes: split it, or run an Express through it.

Depot — spend your mileage

  • Night market: list real-world privileges you want (e.g. "stay up an hour later") and set your own price. Redeeming deducts mileage and sends the request to the tower — it takes effect on approval; declines are refunded.
  • Bounty board: challenges posted by the tower. Claim when you hit the goal; if the reward isn't tempting enough, counter-offer — negotiating is your right.
  • Garage: unlock bus liveries — Violet Storm (3-day streak), Cyber Pink (7-day streak), Pixel Glow (5 express trips), Silver UFO (5,000 mi total).

Signal broadcast — you decide how much to share

ModeWhat the tower sees
Full broadcastEach run's time and ignition record, breakdown alerts
All-clear onlyJust "x/y today ✓" — no details
SilentNothing at all (alerts included)

Keep your records (optional)

Link an Apple ID in Settings. Once linked, pairing, routes and mileage follow you to a new phone. Everything works without linking too.

3. Tower guide

  • Watch dashboard: their runs done today, day streak and total mileage; with Full broadcast, a complete timeline. A breakdown raises a red alert — tap "Got it" to dismiss.
  • Silent signals: three minimal signals — "+1 COOL", "RESPECT", "COPY THAT". One +1 is enough: they know you're there, without the nagging.
  • Bounties: tap "Post" to set a reward + goal. Pick a quantified goal (streak / express trips / on-time ignitions — progress tracks automatically) or a custom task (free text like "do the dishes on Sunday"; they claim it themselves when done — deliver when you see "Completed"). When they counter-offer, accept the new reward or keep the original.
  • Depot approvals: privileges they list show up immediately (Listed); a redeem turns it into a pending approval — approve and it takes effect, decline and the mileage refunds. A deal is a deal.
The tower's spirit is quiet watching: what you can do is deliberately limited — you can't edit their runs or leave lectures. Giving space is also trust.

4. Pairing

  1. Pilot: Settings → Tower link → Generate code, and give the 6 digits to your tower.
  2. Tower: enter a nickname (e.g. Mum) and the code, tap Connect.
  3. Codes are single-use and valid for 24 hours; regenerating invalidates the old one immediately.
  4. Either side can unpair anytime. If the tower leaves, the pilot can generate a new code; if the pilot leaves, the pairing ends.

5. Roles & buddy mode

The tower isn't parents-only — classmates and friends who keep each other honest can be each other's guardians.

  • You can be your own pilot and someone else's tower at the same time — two views, switch freely, neither affects the other.
  • Pilot view: Settings → Watch → Switch to watch view. Tower view: "Switch to my cockpit" at the bottom.
  • The only rule: you cannot be your own tower — mutual watching needs each person's own device and each other's codes.

6. FAQ

Not getting reminders?

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Nightbus. Reminders are scheduled about a week ahead; if the app isn't opened for a while you'll get a "Reminders are asleep" notice — open the app once to top them up.

What happens on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Reinstalling on the same device usually restores your account automatically. For cross-device safety, link an Apple ID in Settings.

Want a fresh start?

Settings → Erase all data: removes everything on the device and signs out; the next launch starts from role selection.

How long until the other side sees my action?

Sync between paired devices normally takes 5–20 seconds, depending on the network.

Is my data safe?

No sign-up, anonymous accounts; only what you choose to share syncs, and only after pairing; no ads, no tracking, no data sale. See the privacy policy.

4. 配对

  1. 驾驶员：设置 → 塔台连接 → 生成配对码，把 6 位数字给你的塔台。
