1. 快速開始

Nightbus 是給任何有 ADHD 特質的人——青少年或成人——的日常提醒。它有兩邊，通常在兩台手機上：

  • 駕駛員——記錄自己日常事項的人。到點收到提醒，點一下完成。
  • 副駕——你選擇讓他看一張卡的人：家人、伴侶或夥伴。只看到你分享的部分，能送你三種正向訊號。
  1. 打開 App——免註冊，直接開始。
  2. 在「你在哪一邊？」選一邊。這只是「先看哪個畫面」，之後隨時可切。
  3. 駕駛員：輸入你的代號（例如 Casper）就出發。副駕：輸入駕駛員給你的 6 位配對碼。
  4. 駕駛員請在提示出現時開啟通知——到點提醒全靠它。
還沒有人配對？副駕的配對碼頁有「示範模式」，單機就能看到那張卡長什麼樣。

2. 駕駛員

主畫面：今天的時間線

今天的事項按時間排成一列。到點那一格會亮起來，其他全部安靜；已完成的沉到底部變暗。頂部七格是接下來七天，只能往後看一週。

  • 點一下那一列 = 點火（完成）。
  • 長按 = 塞車中，延後 15 分鐘，最多兩次。
  • 錯過的班次只是灰的，寫「下一班 下午 8:00」。不會變紅，也不會數你錯過幾次。

新增事項：三個欄位

右下角「＋」。名稱 → 燃料 或 任務 → 每天／每週／只這一次。就這樣。

  • 燃料 = 藥物、保健品。任務 = 其他要做的事。兩者只差圖示，其他完全一樣。
  • 「每天」預設一個時間（第一次是 08:00，之後記住你上次設的），「＋」再加一個時間。
  • 「只這一次」可以不設時間：那是全天事項，整天都能完成，只在 09:00 提醒一次，永遠不會「塞車」。

提醒：到點就響，鎖屏上就能完成

時機會發生什麼
到點通知響（自訂鈴聲，約 25 秒）。內容只有事項名稱和時間。
+5 分、之後每 10 分還沒點火就同一則再響，直到點火或寬限結束（到點後 60 分鐘，每次塞車加 15）。
長按通知「點火」直接完成；「塞車中 · 延後 15 分鐘」。都不用解鎖或開 App。
手機在靜音開關下不會響也不會震，只有橫幅——這是 iOS 的界線，不是 App 的設定。

連續紀錄

主畫面右上角是連續有完成的天數。只有這一個數字；沒有分數、沒有排名。

3. 副駕

副駕的手機上只有一張卡：

今天 3／4 ✓
最後一次 下午 2:20

［ +1 酷 ］［ 尊敬 ］［ 收到 ］

  • 三個按鈕是全部的操作。它們是正向訊號，駕駛員下次打開 App 時，畫面上會浮出一句輕輕的話——是 App 內的訊息，不是通知，不會響。沒有「催」的按鈕。
  • 駕駛員設為「全部」時，點卡可以展開今天的清單：每列顯示時間、名稱、已完成的時間或「塞車中」／「未到站」。
  • 連續兩次沒有點火，卡才會寫「塞車中 · 最後一次 …」。單次不驚動任何人。這一行也是灰的。
  • 塞車中只在副駕打開 App 時看得到；目前不會推播通知。

4. 配對與分享層級

  1. 駕駛員：設定 → 副駕連線 → 產生 6 位配對碼。24 小時內有效、只能用一次。
  2. 副駕：在自己的手機輸入暱稱和那 6 位碼。連上後駕駛員會看到「〈暱稱〉已連線」。

分享多少由駕駛員決定，設定裡三選一，隨時可換：

層級副駕看到
全部那張卡，並可展開今天的清單。
只看總覽只有那張卡（含塞車中），不能展開。
靜音只有「靜音中」。沒有數字、沒有塞車中。

這些層級由伺服器規則強制，不是靠 App 畫面藏起來。任一邊都可以隨時解除配對。

5. 一個帳號、兩邊都能當

你可以一邊當自己的駕駛員，一邊當另一個人的副駕——伴侶、朋友、互相督促的夥伴。

  • 駕駛員畫面：設定 → 「副駕」卡 → 「切到副駕畫面」。
  • 副駕畫面：底部「切到我的駕駛艙」。
  • 切換只是換畫面；你的事項、配對和提醒都不受影響。
  • 一個帳號不能和自己配對——副駕一定是另一台手機上的另一個人。

6. 常見問題

沒有收到提醒？

設定 → 通知 → Nightbus 要開啟，且「時效性通知」為開。App 內設定頁有「通知診斷」卡，能看到下一則提醒的時間。

手機關機或 App 被強制結束時，副駕會看到什麼？

伺服器每 15 分鐘檢查一次；超過寬限沒有點火的班次會被標記，副駕清單上那一列會顯示「塞車中」。這不需要你的手機在線。

重裝 App 會怎樣？

重裝可能遺失紀錄。換手機或重裝前，先在設定用 Apple 帳號「保留你的紀錄」。

我的資料安全嗎？

免註冊、匿名帳號；只在配對後同步你選擇分享的內容；無廣告、無追蹤、不出售資料、不收集健康資料。詳見隱私政策。

1. Quick start

Nightbus is a daily reminder for anyone with ADHD traits — teen or adult. It has two sides, usually on two phones:

  • Pilot — the person keeping their own daily items. Gets reminded when it is time, taps once to finish.
  • Co-pilot — someone you let see one card: family, a partner or a buddy. Sees only what you share and can send you three positive signals.
  1. Open the app — no sign-up, just start.
  2. On "Which side are you on?" pick one. It only decides which screen you see first; you can switch any time.
  3. Pilot: enter your callsign (e.g. Casper) and go. Co-pilot: enter the 6-digit code the pilot gives you.
  4. Pilots: enable notifications when asked — the reminders depend on it.
Nobody to pair with yet? The co-pilot's code screen has a demo mode that shows the card on a single phone.

2. Pilot

Main screen: today's timeline

Today's items in one column, by time. The slot that is due lights up; everything else stays quiet; finished ones sink to the bottom, dimmed. The seven cells on top are the next seven days — you can only look a week ahead.

  • Tap a row = Ignite (done).
  • Long-press = In traffic — delay 15 minutes, at most twice.
  • A missed slot is just grey and says "Next run 8:00 PM". It never turns red and nothing counts your misses.

Adding an item: three fields

The "+" at the bottom right. Name → Fuel or Task → Every day / Weekly / Just once. That is all.

  • Fuel = medication, supplements. Task = anything else to do. They differ only in icon.
  • "Every day" starts with one time (8:00 AM the first time, then whatever you set last); "+" adds another.
  • "Just once" can have no time: an all-day item, doable any time that day, reminded once at 9:00 AM, never "in traffic".

Reminders: it rings, and you finish it on the lock screen

WhenWhat happens
On timeThe notification rings (custom sound, about 25 s). It says only the item's name and time.
+5 min, then every 10The same notification rings again until you ignite or the grace period ends (60 minutes after the time, +15 per "in traffic").
Long-press the notification"Ignite" finishes it; "In traffic · 15 min" delays it. Neither needs unlocking or opening the app.
With the phone's silent switch on there is no sound or vibration, only the banner — that is an iOS boundary, not an app setting.

Streak

Top right of the main screen: the number of consecutive days with at least one check-in. That is the only number — no score, no ranking.

3. Co-pilot

The co-pilot's phone shows one card:

Today 3/4 ✓
Last check-in 2:20 PM

[ +1 cool ] [ Respect ] [ Got it ]

  • The three buttons are the whole interface. They are positive signals; the next time the pilot opens the app, a quiet line appears on screen — an in-app message, not a notification, and it does not ring. There is no nag button.
  • When the pilot shares "Full", tapping the card opens today's list: time, name, and either the check-in time or "In traffic" / "Not due yet".
  • "In traffic · last check-in …" appears on the card only after two consecutive slots go unignited. A single miss disturbs nobody. The line is grey like everything else.
  • "In traffic" is visible when the co-pilot opens the app; there is no push notification for it yet.

4. Pairing and sharing tiers

  1. Pilot: Settings → Co-pilot link → Generate code. Valid 24 hours, single use.
  2. Co-pilot: enter a nickname and the 6 digits. The pilot then sees "〈nickname〉 is connected".

How much is shared is the pilot's choice, three tiers in Settings, switchable any time:

TierThe co-pilot sees
FullThe card, and can open today's list.
Summary onlyThe card only (including "In traffic"); no list.
SilentOnly "Silent". No numbers, no "In traffic".

The tiers are enforced by server rules, not hidden by the app. Either side can unpair at any time.

5. One account, both sides

You can be your own pilot and someone else's co-pilot at the same time — a partner, a friend, a buddy you keep honest.

  • Pilot screen: Settings → the "Co-pilot" card → "Open the co-pilot side".
  • Co-pilot screen: "Back to my cockpit" at the bottom.
  • Switching only changes the screen; your items, pairing and reminders are untouched.
  • An account cannot pair with itself — the co-pilot is always another person on another phone.

6. FAQ

No reminders arriving?

Settings → Notifications → Nightbus must be on, with "Time Sensitive" enabled. The app's Settings page has a "Notification diagnostics" card showing when the next reminder fires.

What does the co-pilot see if my phone is off or the app was force-quit?

A server check runs every 15 minutes; a slot past its grace period without a check-in is marked, and that row shows "In traffic" on the co-pilot's list. Your phone does not need to be online.

What if I reinstall?

Reinstalling may lose your records. Before changing phones or reinstalling, use "Keep your records" with your Apple ID in Settings first.

Is my data safe?

No sign-up, anonymous account; only what you choose to share syncs, and only after pairing; no ads, no tracking, no data sale, no health data. See the privacy policy.

6. 常见问题

没有收到提醒？

设置 → 通知 → Nightbus 要开启，且“时效性通知”为开。App 内设置页有“通知诊断”卡，能看到下一条提醒的时间。

重装 App 会怎样？

我的数据安全吗？

免注册、匿名账号；只在配对后同步你选择分享的内容；无广告、无跟踪、不出售数据、不收集健康数据。详见隐私政策。
